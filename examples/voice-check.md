# The Listening Check

Dr. Morgan checked the reading: 3.14 metres. “Is that right?” she asked.
“Yes,” said Lee, “but listen to the room.”

For a moment, nobody spoke. Rain tapped on the window; somewhere beyond the door, an old radio hummed.

Then a quiet voice began: “We have time. Take a breath, turn the page, and let the next sentence arrive.”
