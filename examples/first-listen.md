# Chapter 1 — A Small Beginning

The workshop was quiet until someone switched on the old radio.
Outside, rain tapped against the window. Inside, a new idea was taking shape.

“What if a book could meet you where you are?” she asked.

# Chapter 2 — A Voice at the Door

You could listen while resting, cooking dinner, or watching the clouds.
You could pause, return to a favourite chapter, or slow the voice down.

The words would still be yours. Now they had another way to reach you.
